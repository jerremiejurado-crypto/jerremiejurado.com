QuickBooks Technical Support — Transaction & Reconciliation Support

Customer challenge

Customers came in with missing money or transactions, reconciliation discrepancies, and account issues that needed to be understood before they could be fixed.

What I investigated

I looked into the specific transactions and financial information involved, tracing what happened and why the numbers didn't match what the customer expected.

How I helped

I guided customers through troubleshooting and resolution steps, assisted with reconciliation-related concerns, helped generate reports, and followed up by email to confirm concerns were properly addressed — escalating complex cases when needed.

Tools / systems

QuickBooks OnlineInternal case toolsEmail follow-up

Outcome

Customers left each case with their transactions clarified and their reconciliation concerns addressed or moving toward resolution.

What I learned

How to stay calm and methodical when a customer's finances feel urgent to them, and how to translate technical detail into steps a non-technical person can follow.

Positioned honestly as QuickBooks Technical Support with exposure to transaction investigation, reconciliation-related workflows, and financial reporting — not as professional bookkeeping or accounting employment. No customer names, account details, or financial figures are shared.
